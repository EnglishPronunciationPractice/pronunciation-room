Reader Profiles

Your profile describes pronunciation, not your English.

After five reviewed readings, your Core, Moderate and Challenging target-word results create a Reader Profile. It can change gradually as your pronunciation record develops.

Voice Builder→Clear Reader→Polished Reader→Expressive Reader→Eloquent Reader

Your Reader Profile reflects pronunciation while reading aloud in The Pronunciation Room. It is not a judgement of your overall English ability.

Progress Score

One private score, four simple ingredients.

45%Word retentionDo previously corrected words stay corrected when they appear again?
30%Attempts to masteryHow many submitted correction rounds do you need before your focus words are approved?
15%ConsistencyHow many of the readings available to you have you completed?
10%LongevityHow long have you kept showing up? Every five completed readings earns one point.

The website calculates this automatically. The full score appears after five readings and enough retention history.

Consistency

Showing up when a reading is available.

If five readings have been available to you and you complete all five, your consistency is 5/5 = 100%. Readings that happened before you joined do not count against you. A reading still open does not count as missed while you still have time to complete it.

Longevity

Recognition for staying with the journey.

Every five completed readings earns one Longevity Point: 5 = 1 point, 10 = 2, 15 = 3, 20 = 4, 25 = 5, and the visible points can keep growing. Only the first five points affect the Progress Score, so long-term members are recognised without becoming unbeatable.

Word retention

Can a corrected word stay corrected?

If comfortable was previously one of your focus words and you mastered it, the system waits until that exact word appears in a later reading. If it is not flagged again, that is a successful retention opportunity. If it needs work again, it simply becomes a focus word again.

Monthly leaderboard

Commitment, not pronunciation ability.

You become eligible after five completed readings. The monthly Top 3 is based on current-month consistency plus a smaller longevity contribution. Your Reader Profile, mistakes, correction attempts and Progress Score do not affect leaderboard rank.

Only the Top 3 positions are shown. If you are outside the Top 3, your rank is not displayed.

Privacy

Your corrections remain yours.

Your email, feedback, focus words, correction attempts, retention history and private charts are visible only to you and the administrator. Community cards use an illustrated avatar and nickname. Real profile photos are not supported.
